Neural Amp Modeler (NAM) Plugin is a VST3 and AudioUnit plugin that runs neural network amp models in real time inside your DAW. Built on the [iPlug2](https://iplug2.github.io) framework, it lets you load `.nam` model files — trained captures of real amplifiers and gear — and play through them with low latency, accurate tone, and full DAW integration.

## What NAM Plugin does

The plugin chains several DSP stages in sequence to give you a full amp-in-a-box signal path:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Input gain and calibration">
    Set the input level to drive the model correctly. Optional input calibration aligns your interface's analog level with the model's expected signal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Noise gate">
    A configurable noise gate silences unwanted hum and hiss when you're not playing, without coloring your tone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="NAM model processing">
    The neural network model runs in real time, resampling automatically if the model was trained at a different sample rate than your session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tone stack EQ">
    A 3-band tone stack (Bass, Mid, Treble) lets you shape the output of the model, just like the EQ section on a real amp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Impulse Response (cabinet sim)">
    Load a `.wav` IR file to add cabinet and microphone coloration after the amp model for a complete, speaker-in-the-room sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Output level and metering">
    Set the final output level with multiple output modes — Raw, Normalized, or Calibrated — and monitor signal with real-time input and output meters.
  </Step>
</Steps>
